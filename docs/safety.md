# Safety notice

The experiments and activities on this website are intended for use by
science teachers and are provided for educational purposes.

Science experiments can involve chemicals, heat, electricity, glassware
and living materials. Before using any lesson from this website:

- Perform experiments only in a properly equipped classroom or
  laboratory, under the supervision of a qualified teacher or laboratory
  technician.
- Follow your school's safety procedures and the regulations that apply
  in your country or state — some substances and procedures described
  here are restricted in some places, or unsuitable for younger
  students.
- Read the [Laboratory Safety](topics/topic03.md) section before
  undertaking practical work, and use protective equipment wherever
  indicated.
- If you are not an experienced teacher, do not attempt these
  experiments on your own.

The lessons reproduce well-established experiments drawn from recognized
educational sources. Even so, conditions, equipment and materials vary,
and all experiments are undertaken at your own risk. Neither Dr John
Elfick nor the people who produce and maintain this website accept
liability for any injury, loss or damage arising from the use of this
material.
